**PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT (PRD)** 

**HelpDesk Lite — Internal Support Ticketing Workspace** 

**1\. PROJECT TITLE** 

HelpDesk Lite — Internal Support Ticketing Workspace

**2\. WHAT? (OBJECTIVE)** 

Build a lightweight internal helpdesk system that allows employees to submit support requests and enables support teams to assign, track, update, and resolve them through one structured workflow. 

**3\. WHY? (BUSINESS VALUE)** 

 At present, employees communicate via email, chats, and informal communication channels for support issues.

The HelpDesk Lite software is expected to minimize duplicate and lost requests, enhance accountability and follow-up, and provide managers with visibility of the work load on support.

**4\. WHO ARE THE USERS?** 

* Employees  
* Support / Operations Staff  
* Managers  
* Product / Operations Stakeholder

**5\. PROBLEM** 

* There are multiple avenues through which support requests can be made at this time.


* This means that there is no clarity in terms of responsibility, follow-up, and knowing whether something is pending, delayed, or completed.

**6\. SOLUTION** 

Build HelpDesk Lite, a central support workspace in which:

Employee

*  Submits a support ticket  
*  Ticket gets added to support queue  
*  Support team takes ownership of ticket  
*  Status of ticket gets updated  
*  Support team closes the ticket  
*  Employee can track the ticket’s progress and closure  
*  Manager can oversee the entire process


**7\. FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS**

* Generate and submit requests for support.  
* Review submitted requests and status.  
* Distribute requests to the support team members.  
* Update the status of requests.  
* Add comments and other details.  
* Upload attachments.  
* Close and resolve requests.  
* Facilitate the viewing of pending requests by the manager.  
* Provide for basic request categorization and priorities.


**8\. NON-FUNCTIONAL REQUIREMENTS**

* User-friendly and intuitive interface.  
* Quick response to user’s requests.  
* Security with proper authentication and authorization.  
* Reliability of data storage.  
* Consistent workflow.  
* Ease of maintenance and scalability.  
* Keep the system simple.

**9\. UI / UX REFERENCES** 

* Figma Design  
* User Flow  
* Wireframes

**10\. DEPENDENCIES** 

* Firebase  
* Internet connection   
* Flutter Framework   
  


**11\. SUCCESS MEASURES**

* Employees find it easy to make requests.  
* Each open request has someone responsible for it.  
* Status of all requests is visible to users.  
* Requests are managed by support teams within one workspace.  
* Managers know about all open requests and workload.  
* Duplicate requests are minimized.
